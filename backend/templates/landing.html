How a call gets stopped

From ringing phone to closed case, in four stages.

01

Signal capture

Call transcript or citizen-reported message enters the detection engine as plain text — no special hardware required.

02

Pattern match

The engine checks for impersonation claims, forced isolation, urgency-to-transfer, and OTP requests — the fingerprint of a digital arrest scam.

03

Risk verdict

A scored, explainable verdict is issued in seconds — safe, suspicious, or confirmed pattern — with the specific red flags named.

04

Network linkage

Flagged numbers, UPI IDs, and devices are added to the fraud graph, connecting this case to others before the ring can move on.
